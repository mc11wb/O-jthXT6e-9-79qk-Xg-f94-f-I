Where to buy Naruto cards in Geneva

Naruto Mythos TCG, launched in March 2026 by Italian publisher Cicaboom, is the Naruto card game we carry. On 13 November, Set 3 brings the Akatsuki and the Shippuden era into the game.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed Naruto Mythos boosters, ten cards a pack, in 1st Edition when we secure it. Set 2, Shinobi Shiren, released on 4 September in Europe; Set 3, Akatsuki, lands on 13 November.

One quirk worth knowing before you buy: Cicaboom publishes each set in two editions. The first is never reprinted, the second arrives roughly two months later with a stamp, different packaging and different Gold and Secret Rare cards. Collectors chase the first, players are perfectly served by the second.

Sets and editions

  • Set 3 — Akatsuki13 November 2026, first Shippuden set
  • Set 2 — Shinobi ShirenReleased 4 September 2026
  • Set 1 — Konoha ShidōFounding set, March 2026

Why Set 3 changes everything

Until now Naruto Mythos was a European game: launch limited to Europe, the UK, Turkey and the Middle East. Akatsuki is the game's first simultaneous worldwide release, with the United States, Canada, Latin America, Australia and South Africa included from day one. In practice, demand on this set will be on another scale, and the limited edition will not keep up.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Naruto cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton: Rive, Les Charmilles, Petit-Lancy, Plainpalais, Meyrin, Thônex, Carouge and King Jouet Carouge. The Live Stock page shows rail by rail what is left.
Which Naruto card game do you sell?
Naruto Mythos TCG, published by Cicaboom, as factory-sealed boosters from the official channel.
What is the difference between 1st and 2nd Edition?
The 1st Edition is never reprinted and carries the original numbered chase cards. The 2nd arrives about two months later, with a stamp, different packaging and different Gold cards.
When does Set 3 Akatsuki release?
13 November 2026, with prereleases on the 7th. It is the game's first simultaneous worldwide launch.
Are the products genuine?
Yes, factory-sealed and sourced from official distributors.
