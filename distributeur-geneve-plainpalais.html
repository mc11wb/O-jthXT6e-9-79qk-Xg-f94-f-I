    Frequently asked questions

    Where can I buy Pokémon cards in Genève?
    BoosterStop runs seven vending machines across the canton of Geneva, including this one in Genève Plainpalais, Rue Pictet-de-Bock 6. They are self-service during the opening hours of the branch hosting them.
    How do I pay?
    Card, contactless and mobile payment. The machine does not take cash.
    Are the cards genuine?
    Yes. Every product comes from official distributors, in its original sealed packaging.
    Is the displayed stock reliable?
    It is read straight from the machine. A product can still sell out in the minutes after you check.
    The machine did not dispense my product — what now?
    Write to us with the location and the time. We refund or replace after checking the machine's log.
