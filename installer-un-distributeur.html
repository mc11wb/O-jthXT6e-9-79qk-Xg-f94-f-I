Host a vending machine in your venue

BoosterStop installs trading card vending machines in high-traffic locations across the canton of Geneva. You provide the spot, we handle the rest.

Our machines are installed inside seven Swiss Post branches across the canton of Geneva. Go and see one before you write to us: all seven addresses are on the machines page.

Who does what

  • Every week, and more often when a new set drops. Restocking is handled by our team, there is nothing for you to manage.

What you provide

What it brings your venue

A card machine draws a returning crowd: collectors come back with every new set, and our live stock page gives them a reason to check before they travel. That is extra footfall for you, not just a machine in a corner.

What people ask us

How am I paid?
Two models are possible: a fixed rent, or a share of the revenue the machine generates. We agree together on whichever suits your venue and its footfall.
Who handles restocking?
Every week, and more often when a new set drops. Restocking is handled by our team, there is nothing for you to manage.
How do customers pay?
Cash with change returned in coins and notes, TWINT, contactless card and smartphone. Nothing goes through your own till.
Who handles installation and breakdowns?
Installation, refilling, maintenance and customer service are entirely handled by BoosterStop.
How much space does it take?
Around 1 m² of floor space, or 1.1 m² including the clearance needed. It weighs 280 kg.
What does the machine need?
A standard power socket, nothing else. Consumption is very low.
Who owns the machine?
It remains our property and is covered by our insurance. You carry no financial responsibility for it.
How long is the commitment?
Flexible, with no minimum term imposed. We agree it with you according to your situation.
