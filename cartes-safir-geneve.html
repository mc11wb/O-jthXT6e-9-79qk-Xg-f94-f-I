Where to buy Safir Cards in Geneva

No external licence, no Japanese or American publisher behind it: Safir is an original world, designed and drawn by French-speaking artists. It is the only licence in our machines you will not find in any other vending machine in the canton.

What sits in the rails

Sealed Safir boosters, season 3, Les Trois Royaumes, first, and the Nuit Blanche themed expansion as shipments allow. The game plays across three zones, with heroes and spells, and its rules are written in French, not translated.

It is also one of the few licences where we speak directly to the publisher. New releases arrive without a middleman and without import delay.

Expansions we carry

  • Season 3 — Les Trois RoyaumesCurrent expansion
  • Nuit BlancheThemed expansion

Why back an independent TCG

The big licences live on recognition: you buy Pikachu or Luffy before you buy a game. Safir lives on the opposite, a world you discover through the cards. The community is small but grows season by season, and it is mostly French-Swiss and French. For a Geneva player, it is the licence where an opponent who lives next door is easiest to find.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Safir Cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton. The Live Stock page shows what is left in each machine.
Is Safir a game or a collection?
A complete game, with a three-zone combat system, heroes and spells, and a community that plays.
Are the cards in French?
Yes, natively. Safir is designed in French.
Who publishes Safir?
An independent French-speaking publisher, with no external licence or group behind it.
How do I find players?
The Safir community runs its own meetups; links are on the publisher’s site.
