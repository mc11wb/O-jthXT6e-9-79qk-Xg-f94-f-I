Where to buy NHL hockey cards in Geneva

Geneva is a hockey town, and Upper Deck has held the exclusive NHL licence for decades. Series 1 of the 2026-27 season lands this autumn with its fifty Young Guns, the rookies everyone hunts.

What sits in the rails

Sealed Upper Deck Series 1 then Series 2 packs, the NHL’s flagship product. The base set has 200 cards; the Young Guns, numbered 201 to 250, fall one pack in two and carry all the product’s value.

Upper Deck is the only publisher allowed to use NHL logos and jerseys, which makes the choice simple: for licensed hockey, it is Upper Deck or nothing.

Young Guns, the card that matters

Since 1990 a player’s Young Guns has been his reference rookie card, the one that gets graded and handed down. A player who breaks through sees his Young Guns gain value for his whole career. That is why Series 1 is awaited every autumn, in Switzerland too, where the league’s Swiss players have collectors of their own.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy NHL cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton. The Live Stock page shows which collections are in the rails.
When does Upper Deck Series 1 2026-27 release?
This autumn; the exact date goes into our release calendar as soon as Upper Deck confirms it.
What is a Young Guns card?
Upper Deck’s official rookie card, numbered 201 to 250 in Series 1, one pack in two. It has been the market reference since 1990.
Are there Swiss players?
Yes, the NHL’s Swiss players appear in Upper Deck collections like everyone else.
Are the packs sealed?
Yes, factory-sealed and sourced from official distributors.
