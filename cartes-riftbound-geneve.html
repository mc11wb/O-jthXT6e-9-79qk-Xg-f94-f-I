Where to buy Riftbound cards in Geneva

Riot Games launched its physical card game in late 2025 and it went straight into the global top five by sales. In Geneva you will find it as sealed boosters in our machines.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed Riftbound boosters. The game brings the League of Legends universe into cards, with nearly forty playable champions from the first set and a quarterly release pace.

It is the newest licence we carry, and the hardest to find on a shelf in Geneva: specialist shops clear it as soon as it arrives. Our rails are refilled with every wave.

Why it took off so fast

League of Legends brings an enormous player base already familiar with the champions and the regions of Runeterra. The game never had to build its universe, only its mechanics. The result is a faster start than any other new licence of the decade.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Riftbound cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton, from Rive to King Jouet Carouge. The Live Stock page shows what is left in each connected machine.
What exactly is Riftbound?
Riot Games' official collectible card game, based on the League of Legends universe, launched in late 2025.

No. Knowing the champions helps, but the card game stands on its own.
