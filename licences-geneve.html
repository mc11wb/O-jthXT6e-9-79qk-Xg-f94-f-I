Frequently asked questions

What we get asked most

Why does BoosterStop carry so many different card licenses in Geneva?

Our goal is to give as many licenses as possible a real home, from the great classics to the most specialist imports like Gundam Card Game, Riftbound, Disney Lorcana and Cyberpunk TCG, so players and collectors can find everything in one place, 24/7.

How do I check live stock for a specific license before going to a machine?

Each license card on this page shows a live stock indicator connected to our machines. You can also check the full dashboard at any time.

A license I'm looking for isn't available yet, what can I do?

Use the request button on that license's card to let us know. We track every request to prioritize sourcing, especially for brand-new games like Cyberpunk TCG or the upcoming official Naruto Card Game.

Are BoosterStop's less common and niche products authentic and sealed?

Yes. Every product, from a classic Pokémon booster to a niche import like Azuki TCG or Safir Cards, is 100% sealed and sourced from official distributors. Nothing is opened, weighed or hand-picked before it reaches the machine.

Which are the least common licenses in BoosterStop's catalog?

Gundam Card Game, Riftbound: League of Legends TCG, Cyberpunk TCG, Naruto, the Magic: The Gathering Universes Beyond crossovers (Final Fantasy, Marvel, The Hobbit), Azuki TCG, Vibes TCG and the independent French game Safir Cards are the least common in our selection.

Have a license in mind that's not listed here?

Tell us. That's how Azuki TCG and Vibes TCG ended up on this list. We keep an eye on everything new that launches.
