Cyberpunk TCG in Geneva: where to find it at release

Twenty-eight million dollars raised on Kickstarter, the category record. The first physical card game of the Cyberpunk 2077 and Edgerunners universe is not out yet; we will carry it in Geneva the day it is.

What the launch set will be

Welcome to Night City, the founding set, brings together V, Johnny Silverhand and Judy Álvarez, with the look of the video game and the Netflix series. CD PROJEKT RED develops, WeirdCo publishes.

We track the release through our official distributor. The day the set is available, it is in the rails. Until then, the request button on the licences page tells us how many people are waiting for it in Geneva, and that sets the allocation we ask for.

What is announced

  • Welcome to Night CityLaunch set, date pending

Why such excitement before release

The Kickstarter showed the Cyberpunk community wanted a physical object, not only a game. The cards reuse the studio’s artists’ work, and the game system was publicly tested for a year. Few TCGs arrive with a player base already in place.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cyberpunk TCG available in Geneva?
Not yet. It will be in our machines on release day, through our official distributor.

The retail date is not yet set. We will publish it here and in our release calendar as soon as it is official.
