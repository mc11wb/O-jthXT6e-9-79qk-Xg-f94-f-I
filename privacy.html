Privacy Policy & Cookies

Last updated: August 2026 — BoosterStop / 247 Distribution SNC, Geneva, Switzerland

1. Who we are

BoosterStop is a trading name of 247 Distribution SNC, based in Geneva, Switzerland. We operate vending machines dedicated to Trading Card Games and this website, boosterstop.com. For any privacy question: contact@boosterstop.ch.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form: first name, last name, email, subject and message — used only to answer you.
  • Newsletter: your email address — used to send you our newsletter and your promo code.
  • Anonymous traffic statistics on this website.

Payments at our machines are processed by certified payment providers. We never see or store your card details.

3. Legal basis & compliance

We process your data in compliance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We only process data you give us voluntarily (consent) or data needed to answer your requests (legitimate interest).

4. Third-party services

  • Formspree (USA) — processes our contact and newsletter form submissions.
  • Brevo (EU) — stores our newsletter subscriber list and sends our emails.
  • Google Maps (USA) — displays the machine location maps and sets its own cookies when loaded.
  • Google Fonts (USA) — loads the fonts used on this site.
  • GitHub Pages (USA) — hosts this website.

5. Cookies

We use a strictly minimal setup: one localStorage entry to remember your cookie choice, and the cookies set by Google Maps when the map loads. No advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking.

6. Retention

Contact messages are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe — every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data at any time by writing to contact@boosterstop.ch. In Switzerland you can also contact the FDPIC; in the EU, your local data protection authority.
