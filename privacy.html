Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 20 July 2026

247 Distribution SNC, based in Geneva, Switzerland.

1. What we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Contact form: first name, last name, email, profile type and message.
  • Newsletter: email, selected countries and games, marketing consent (with timestamp).
  • Restock / product requests: email, machine and product concerned, notes.
  • Technical data: basic browser information to display maps and secure forms.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your messages and partnership enquiries.
  • To send you the emails you signed up for — only with your consent.
  • To process your restock and product requests.
  • To run and secure the website.

3. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies. Essential ones run maps and protect forms; optional ones help us improve the site. You choose via the cookie banner.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use trusted providers:

  • Formspree — form processing.
  • Google Maps — location maps.
  • Brevo — newsletter & marketing emails.
  • Hosting — serves the website.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed, e.g. until you unsubscribe. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

6. Your rights

Under the Swiss nFADP and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Request deletion.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Object to certain processing.

To exercise these, email contact@boosterstop.ch. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.
