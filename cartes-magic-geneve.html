Where to buy Magic cards in Geneva

The first collectible card game ever made, and still number one worldwide by sales value in 2026. Boosters come out sealed from our machines, in Geneva as in Carouge.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed boosters, including the Universes Beyond crossovers that reshaped the licence over the past two years: Final Fantasy, Marvel, The Lord of the Rings. They bring more new players to Magic than anything else right now, and they are what people ask us for most.

Wizards of the Coast keeps a heavy release pace, with several major sets a year. We track standard releases rather than premium Commander products, which suit a shop purchase better.

Universes we carry

  • Magic × Final FantasyThe record-breaking crossover
  • Magic × MarvelSuperheroes in a Magic frame
  • Magic × The Lord of the RingsMiddle-earth, a cult collection

A market of its own

Magic does not behave like anime TCGs. Value is not concentrated in a handful of illustrated cards but spread across playable rares, some of which stay in demand years after release. It is also the licence Geneva players buy most regularly, in small amounts and often.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Magic cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton, from Rive to King Jouet Carouge. The Live Stock page shows what is left in each connected machine.
Do you carry the Universes Beyond crossovers?
Yes, and they are the most requested: Final Fantasy, Marvel and The Lord of the Rings appear in the rails regularly.
Do you sell Magic singles?
No. The machines dispense factory-sealed product only, never singles or repackaged lots.
Can I pay without cash?
Yes, by TWINT or contactless, card or phone. The product drops immediately.
What if the set I want is not there?
Flag it with the request button on the licences page. Requests feed the restock directly.
