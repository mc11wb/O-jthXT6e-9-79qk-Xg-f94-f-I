Where to buy NFL cards in Geneva

America’s most-followed league has a smaller but very loyal audience in Europe, and Geneva, with its expatriates, has more than its share. Prizm and Donruss Football are in our eight machines.

What sits in the rails

Sealed Panini Prizm and Donruss Football packs. The 2026 NFL season kicked off in early September; Donruss comes first, Prizm over winter, with April’s draft rookies in everyone’s sights.

The NFL market stands out for a very active secondary market in autographs, even more than the NBA. Retail packs hold few; the main draw remains rookie quarterbacks, whose value can change in a single Sunday.

  • Panini Prizm NFLThe American football reference
  • Panini Donruss NFLRated Rookies, season opener

A market that moves on Sundays

Unlike TCGs, an NFL card’s value moves with results. A rookie quarterback who nails his first game sees his Prizm double within the week. That is what keeps the collection alive for those who follow the league, and why a pack bought on Friday is not worth the same on Monday.
