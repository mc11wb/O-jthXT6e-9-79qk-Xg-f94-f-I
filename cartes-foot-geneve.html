Where to buy football cards in Geneva

It is a World Cup year, and the rails show it. Match Attax and Adrenalyn XL have been the two fastest-moving collections in the network since spring.

The two collections in the machines

Topps Match Attax covers the European club competitions, Champions League, Europa League and Conference League, with the rarity system children know by heart. The 2026-27 edition, released on 10 September, marks twenty years of the collection and introduces full-art Mystical cards.

Alongside it, Panini Adrenalyn XL World Cup 2026 follows the national teams. It is the playground trading collection, the one that brings people back to a machine again and again.

Why a vending machine for football cards

A football card pack is an impulse buy of a few francs, rarely planned. The corner kiosk has always covered that, except it closes and its stock is invisible. Our machines sit inside Swiss Post branches, on routes families already take, and the stock can be checked online before the trip.
