Where to buy Topps cards in Geneva

Topps is not a licence, it is the publisher that holds them. Behind a Match Attax pack or a Chrome F1 card sits the same house, owned by Fanatics since 2022.

The Topps sub-licences in our machines

Topps has produced the official cards of UEFA's club competitions since 2015, of the Bundesliga since 2008, of MLS, and of the Premier League since the 2025-26 season. On the motorsport side it also holds the Formula 1 licence, plus MLB and UFC internationally.

In our Geneva rails two families dominate: Match Attax, the mass-market format built for trading, and Chrome, the premium line with holographic parallels whose secondary market is the most active.

Topps lines we carry

  • Match Attax UEFA Club CompetitionsChampions, Europa and Conference League
  • Topps Turbo Attax F1European, mass-market format
  • Topps Chrome Formula 1Drivers, teams, autographs

Match Attax or Chrome, which to pick

Match Attax is made to be played and traded, with a rarity system an eight-year-old can read. The 2026-27 edition, out on 10 September, celebrates twenty years of the line and adds full-art Mystical cards. Chrome targets the collector: limited print runs, numbered parallels, autographs. Two audiences, two budgets, and both in the machines.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Topps cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton: Rive, Les Charmilles, Petit-Lancy, Plainpalais, Meyrin, Thônex, Carouge and King Jouet Carouge.
Which licences does Topps hold?
UEFA club competitions, the Bundesliga, MLS, the Premier League since 2025-26, Formula 1, plus MLB and UFC internationally.
What is the difference between Match Attax and Topps Chrome?
Match Attax is a mass-market format designed for playing and trading. Chrome is a premium line with holographic parallels, aimed at collectors.
Is Topps still owned by the Shorin family?
No. Topps was bought by Fanatics in 2022, after belonging to Tornante and Madison Dearborn since 2007.
Are the packs sealed?
Yes, factory-sealed and sourced from official distributors.
