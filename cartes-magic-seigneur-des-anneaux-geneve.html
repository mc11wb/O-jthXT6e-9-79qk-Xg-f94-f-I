Where to buy Magic × Lord of the Rings cards in Geneva

Bilbo arrived on 14 August. After Tales of Middle-earth and its One Ring in 2023, Magic returns to Middle-earth with The Hobbit, and our rails followed the same day.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed The Hobbit Play Boosters, the set of the moment, with cards transcribed in Dwarvish and a Smaug that is already the most chased card of the set. Tales of Middle-earth comes back into the rails whenever we secure more.

Middle-earth is the only Universes Beyond licence to have had two full sets. The first established the format, the second extends it with the historic artists of the Tolkien fandom.

Sets we carry

  • The HobbitReleased 14 August 2026
  • Tales of Middle-earth2023, the One Ring

The readers’ crossover

Final Fantasy brought video game players, Marvel brought comic readers. Tolkien brings an older crowd, often former Magic players from the nineties coming back for the world. It is the Magic licence with the highest average basket at our machines.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.
