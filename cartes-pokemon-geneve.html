Where to buy Pokémon cards in Geneva

The world’s best-selling card game, in eight machines spread across the canton. It is the licence BoosterStop started with, the one with the most rails, and the one we restock most often.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed boosters, mostly in French, from The Pokémon Company’s official distributors. The current block is Mega Evolution, and the 30th-anniversary set, 30th Celebration, came out on 16 September with a Pikachu in every pack.

Each machine has several Pokémon rails, which lets us keep two or three sets side by side: the latest release, a recent set still in demand, and often a nostalgia set such as 151.

  • Mega Evolution: Delta Reign6 November 2026, Mega Rayquaza ex
  • 151Nostalgia set still in demand

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Pokémon cards in Geneva?
In the eight BoosterStop machines across the canton: Rive, Les Charmilles, Petit-Lancy, Plainpalais, Meyrin, Thônex, Carouge and King Jouet Carouge. The Live Stock page shows rail by rail what is left.
Are the boosters in French?
Mostly, yes. Each rail’s language is shown on the Live Stock page and on the machine screen before payment.
Is a new set available on release day?
Yes. We are supplied by the official distributors and new releases go into the rails as soon as they are out.
Do you sell Pokémon singles?
No. Factory-sealed product only, never singles or repackaged lots.
Which machine is nearest me?
The nearest machine to your neighbourhood section on this page covers every area from Champel to Vernier, with walking and cycling times.
