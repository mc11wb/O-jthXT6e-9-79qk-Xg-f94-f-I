Where to buy Vibes TCG cards in Geneva

Pudgy Penguins as a card game, deliberately absurd, with a collecting scene that is already serious: a season 1 box resells today for 500 to 700 dollars. It is in our rails in Geneva.

What sits in the rails

Sealed Vibes boosters, from the Enter the Huddle set. The game has no mass-market European distribution: we bring it in ourselves, which makes it one of the hardest licences to find anywhere else in the canton.

Quantities stay modest and the price follows the market of the day, shown on the machine before payment. The Live Stock page shows which machine holds the rail, and the request button tells us whether to reorder.

Set concerned

  • Enter the HuddleSet 1, season 1

Why a penguin game costs 700 dollars a box

Pudgy Penguins is one of the few internet-born brands to have made the leap to physical products, from plush toys in US mass retail to a card game. The first set was a short print, the community is loyal, and the secondary market did the rest. It is not a game to start with, it is a collectible.
