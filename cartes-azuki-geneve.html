Where to buy Azuki TCG cards in Geneva

Ten thousand characters hand-drawn by Overwatch’s former art director, a community already running regional tournaments in the United States, and a Europe only just discovering it. It is in our rails in Geneva.

What sits in the rails

Sealed Gates Awakened boosters, the AZK-01 founding set. It is one of the few licences in our machines you will not find on a shelf in Geneva: the game has no mass-market European distribution, we bring it in ourselves.

It is a discovery licence rather than a volume one: one or two rails, not ten. The Live Stock page shows which machine holds it, and the request button on the licences page tells us whether to order more.

Available set

  • Gates Awakened — AZK-01Founding set

A TCG born from a community

Azuki was an illustration collection before it was a game. The move to a TCG kept what made it strong, the style and coherence of the world, and added a game system that holds up. Over a million dollars in preorders for a first set, with no known licence behind it, is rare.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Yes, factory-sealed and sourced through the publisher's official channel.
