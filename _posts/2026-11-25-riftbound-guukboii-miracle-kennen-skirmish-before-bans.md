Just before Riftbound's November 23 bans took effect, [Frites Esports Club](https://www.fec.gg/) player Guukboii brought Miracle Kennen to a local 16-player Skirmish for one more tournament. He finished with the recorded win, but the most memorable match of the day ended in a draw.

His [Skirmish decklist on Rift Atlas](https://play.riftatlas.com/p/9Q2874) (code 9Q2874) stayed close to the one he brought to the [European Championship in Stuttgart](/blog/riftbound-eu-regionals-guukboii-kennen-comeback.html). He replaced [Existential Dread](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-134-existential-dread/) with a second [Edge of Night](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-139-edge-of-night/) and moved one [Ride the Wind](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-173-ride-the-wind/) to the sideboard to make room for a second [Star-Crossed](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-128-star-crossed/), which he considered better against Riven. Most of his early matches were fairly straightforward, with small mistakes by his opponents giving him opportunities to take control. Round two was different.

## Forty minutes for one game

Facing another Kennen player, Guukboii opened without the Seals or [Lightning Rush](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-156-lightning-rush/) plays he wanted. His opponent had a stronger start and threatened to snowball with Lightning Rush from the trash. On turn three, Guukboii used [Rebuke](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-172-rebuke/) on the opposing Kennen to stop the pressure, recycling two runes in the process.

That decision kept him in the game, but it also led to a long stalemate. Each player held one battlefield and scored just one point per turn. Eventually, both reached seven points. They could break each other's holds, but neither could find a way to finish.

Guukboii finally made it through his deck and reached [Nocturne, Horrifying](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-194-nocturne-horrifying/), a card he had put back during his mulligan and tracked in his notes. With Nocturne on the field, a Ride the Wind play let him conquer both battlefields and take game one. It had lasted 40 minutes.

There was barely any time left for the rest of the match. Both players agreed to play quickly so they could try to fit in a third game, but game two became another grind. His opponent found the winning play with only 30 seconds left on the clock. No time remained for a decider, which left the match at 1-1, Guukboii's first genuine draw.

## Two wins and a split final

Guukboii went on to win the next two rounds. A fifth scheduled round never took place because of a problem with the tournament software, so every player received a draw.

In the semifinal against Vex, Guukboii got to play first thanks to his higher seed. Both games followed a similar pattern: each player held one battlefield until a sequence of Equipment plays allowed him to break through the opposing hold in a single turn. He took the semifinal 2-0.

The final would have been a rematch against his Kennen opponent from round two. Instead, the two agreed to split the final, with Guukboii receiving the recorded tournament win. It was not a final played out to a victory, but it was a winning finish to a small local event.

With [Rhasa the Sunderer](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-195-rhasa-the-sunderer/) and Nocturne now banned under [Riot's November 23 update](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/announcements/radiance-ban-list-updates-effective-november-23-2026/), Guukboii believes Miracle Kennen in this form is very likely finished. The small Skirmish gave him one last outing with the deck, and a 40-minute mirror match to remember.
