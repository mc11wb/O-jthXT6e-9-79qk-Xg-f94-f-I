Hunters' Circle is a battlefield from **Riftbound Radiance**, the fifth set of Riot Games' League of Legends trading card game, released on 23 October 2026. It reduces the cost of the first unit or Gear each player plays there on their turn, which could help aggressive decks develop their board faster and get more out of every resource. With synergies around [Ambush](https://runeweave.com/keywords/ambush) units and cards like Sneaky Deckhand, could Hunters' Circle open the door to new Riftbound strategies?

## What Hunters' Circle does

| Hunters' Circle | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Battlefield |
| **Set** | Radiance (RAD 163/167) |
| **Text** | For each player, the first unit or Gear they play here on their turn costs 1 less. |

## Why one energy matters

Reducing a card's cost by a single energy might not look like much, especially late in the game. But every player starts with **only two energy**, so the reduction is far more significant in the early turns. Being able to play a stronger unit before your opponent is ready to respond can give you a huge advantage.

## Sneaky Deckhand is back

In Set 2, [Obelisk of Power](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-284-obelisk-of-power/) was banned because it allowed both players to start the game with an extra rune. The first player could immediately score a point on turn 1 by playing [Sneaky Deckhand](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-176-sneaky-deckhand/), while the second player had no runes available to respond.

With Hunters' Circle, this play pattern appears to be making a comeback. Instead of handing out an extra rune, the battlefield reduces Sneaky Deckhand's cost enough to play it for **two energy and score a point on turn 1** when going first.

If your opponent cannot deal with your unit, you can potentially use Hunters' Circle again on turn 2. That lets you develop for **eight energy across your first two turns instead of six**, while also scoring two points: a massive early-game advantage that is hard to recover from.

## Astral Heron could be even scarier

Sneaky Deckhand isn't the only card that benefits from this battlefield. [Astral Heron](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-044-astral-heron/) is already a very strong unit, but its seven-energy cost normally means you can only play it on turn 4 when going first.

Hunters' Circle lets you bring Astral Heron onto the board **as early as turn 3**, potentially before your opponent has properly developed their board. If the Heron survives another turn, the advantage can quickly snowball out of control. Playing such a powerful unit ahead of schedule puts enormous pressure on your opponent to find an answer.

## Will Hunters' Circle define the meta?

Hunters' Circle looks like one of the most impactful battlefields in Radiance. A one-energy discount sounds small, but the difference it makes in the early game is enormous. We think it enables some very strong strategies, and we are curious to see how players use it.
