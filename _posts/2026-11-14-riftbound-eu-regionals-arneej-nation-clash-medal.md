Arneej arrived at the Riftbound European Championship in Stuttgart ready to compete with Master Yi. His main event started with a strong win, but several close games eventually left him just short of qualifying for Day 2.

However, that was not the end of his weekend. The Frites Esports Club player returned on Saturday for Nation Clash, where he had another opportunity to put his [Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogs-019-master-yi-wuju-bladesman/) deck to the test.

You can check out his [Master Yi decklist on Riftdecks](https://riftdecks.com/riftbound-metagame/deck-master-yi-wuju-bladesman-345205), or see the [decklist Arneej shared on X](https://x.com/ArneejTCG/status/2120261157005275338).

Here is how his weekend unfolded.

## Day 1: a close main event run

### Round 1: PKPikmin (Orianna), win 2-0

Arneej opened his tournament against PKPikmin's Orianna in a feature match.

It was a strong start for the Master Yi player, who secured a clean 2-0 victory without dropping a game. With his first match won, Arneej headed into Round 2 hoping to continue that momentum.

### Round 2: Opettino (Riven), loss 0-2

Round 2 brought a much tougher matchup against Opettino's Riven.

In **Game 1**, Arneej won the dice roll but struggled to build an advantage. His opponent had [Defy](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-045-defy/) ready for a crucial base kill before developing pressure with [Astral Heron](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-044-astral-heron/), leaving Arneej unable to defend effectively.

**Game 2** also ended in defeat. Looking back, Arneej identified a mistake involving [Rengar, Trophy Hunter](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-120-rengar-trophy-hunter/). He had played around the possibility of his opponent drawing Defy, a decision that ultimately did not work out.

### Round 3: keyistyq (LeBlanc), loss 1-2

Arneej's third match was against keyistyq's LeBlanc, in what became one of his closest series of the day.

**Game 1** went quickly in his favour after finding two base kills. In **Game 2**, Arneej was ahead for much of the game, but the situation changed when keyistyq played [Rift Herald](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-179-rift-herald/). Arneej took a risk, hoping to draw [Charm](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-043-charm/) for lethal. Unfortunately, he did not find it.

**Game 3** was another difficult battle. His opponent matched [Windswept Hillock](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-297-windswept-hillock/) against Arneej's [Grove of the God-Willow](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-280-grove-of-the-god-willow/), making an early [Vi, Destructive](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-036-vi-destructive/) troublesome. Arneej cleared Vi and another unit with [Rampage](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-083-rampage/) on Master Yi, but [Ruin Runner](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-105-ruin-runner/) arrived two turns later.

The deciding moment came when Arneej had to hope his opponent did not have [Deathgrip](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-163-deathgrip/) for Rift Herald. Unfortunately, keyistyq had it. Afterwards, Arneej felt he could have played Game 2 better to give himself more chances to win the series.

### Round 4: nUitWlTuiAZEWIKX (Riven), win 2-0

Arneej faced another Riven player in Round 4, but this time the matchup went much better.

In **Game 1**, he found both Charm and Rampage. His opponent did not have Defy, allowing Arneej to secure an 8-0 victory.

**Game 2** was much closer, with both players working with limited runes. At one point, Arneej had to respond to his opponent's [Hush](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-041-hush/) with a Hush of his own to protect a [Punch First](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-097-punch-first/) play.

His opponent also failed to find Astral Heron, helping Arneej maintain control and secure the 2-0 victory.

### Round 5: bsweitz (Kennen), win 2-1

Arneej's fifth round was against bsweitz's Kennen.

**Game 1** was close, but an important combination involving Marauder and Rampage allowed Arneej to deal with Kennen early and take the opening game.

**Game 2** went very differently. His opponent opened with two Seals into Captain and [Nocturne, Horrifying](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-194-nocturne-horrifying/) on turn one, leaving Arneej with little room to recover.

In **Game 3**, Arneej found the base kills he needed and managed to hold Grove for several turns, building enough of an advantage to secure the series.

With a 3-2 record, he was now one victory away from qualifying for Day 2.

### Round 6: Dynamic (Kai'Sa), loss 1-2

Arneej's final opponent was Dynamic, playing Kai'Sa. With qualification still within reach, everything came down to this match.

**Game 1** was closer than the result might suggest. Arneej quickly reached six points, putting himself in a promising position. Dynamic then used [Thousand-Tailed Watcher](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-116-thousand-tailed-watcher/) and [Bilgewater Tracker](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-003-bilgewater-tracker/) to remove two of his units.

However, this was a risky play for Dynamic. If Arneej already had a pump spell in hand, he could have won on [Star Spring](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-215-star-spring/) the following turn by combining it with another unit. Unfortunately, Arneej did not have the pump spell he needed, and Dynamic's gamble paid off.

Arneej recovered in **Game 2**, where Grove helped him secure his only victory of the series. However, **Game 3** was another difficult battle. Without Grove, he struggled to keep up with Kai'Sa's value and could not find Ruin Runner or Hush at the moments he needed them.

Dynamic eventually took the deciding game, ending Arneej's main event run with a 3-3 record and 9 match points, just short of the 12 required to advance.

## Nation Clash: a second chance in Stuttgart

Missing Day 2 was disappointing for Arneej, but his weekend was far from over. On Saturday, November 7, he entered the Nation Clash side tournament with Master Yi, ready for another eight rounds.

### Round 1: Ghu (Riven), win 2-0

Arneej started Nation Clash with a strong performance against Ghu's Riven. He found the base kills he needed in both games. Game 2 turned into a longer battle for resources, but Ghu never found Astral Heron to put Arneej under serious pressure.

The result was a clean 2-0 victory.

### Round 2: Grutzbeutel (Leona), win 2-1

Round 2 brought a close series against Grutzbeutel's Leona deck, featuring an unusual combination of green Riven cards and Kayle. Arneej quickly discovered that an empowered Kayle could be particularly difficult for Master Yi to handle.

The match came down to **Game 3**, where Arneej took a significant risk. He used Charm and Rengar to move Kayle from [Risen Altar](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-163-risen-altar/) to his Grove, hoping the hidden card attached to it was not [Zhonya's Hourglass](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-077-zhonyas-hourglass/).

His gamble worked. The hidden card turned out to be [Back Off](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-042-back-off/), which went to the trash. With Kayle dealt with, Arneej was able to close the deciding game and win the series 2-1.

### Round 3: Tuneexbll (Kai'Sa), win 2-0

Against Tuneexbll's Kai'Sa, Arneej found an important Ruin Runner in **Game 1**. Kai'Sa struggled to deal with it, allowing him to take the opening game.

**Game 2** was closer, but Arneej made good use of his pump spells alongside [Abandoned Hall](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-205-abandoned-hall/) to survive his opponent's removal.

He secured another 2-0 victory, bringing his Nation Clash record to 3-0.

### Round 4: Venom (Riven), win 2-0

Arneej faced another Riven player in Round 4, with a familiar pattern deciding both games. He found the base kills he needed, while Venom failed to draw Defy to stop them.

The result was a second consecutive clean victory, extending Arneej's winning streak to four matches.

### Round 5: GN Malediction (Kennen), loss 1-2

Arneej's fifth match was against GN Malediction's Kennen, his toughest Nation Clash opponent so far.

In **Game 1**, Arneej later considered whether saving Punch First for Ruin Runner and allowing Kennen to reach seven points might have been the better decision. That alternative could have left him in a stronger position to establish a hold afterwards.

**Game 2** went much better. Arneej found early removal for Kennen and successfully held Grove, giving him a relatively straightforward victory.

However, **Game 3** proved difficult. Although Malediction did not find any Lightning Rushes, Arneej's draws were also poor. He struggled to find enough resources during the midgame, allowing his opponent to gain value through Last Rites and [Edge of Night](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-139-edge-of-night/).

The 1-2 loss ended Arneej's unbeaten start, leaving him at 4-1.

### Round 6: CTCG Zrob (Riven), win 2-1

Arneej's sixth match against CTCG Zrob had a difficult opening and an unusual deciding game.

In **Game 1**, Arneej drew five or six two-cost units, leaving him with a hand that struggled to keep up. **Game 2** went much better, as he was able to hold Grove and generate plenty of value.

The deciding **Game 3** included an unexpected distraction. A loud judge call nearby interrupted the match, and Arneej felt it contributed to his opponent making a mistake. Zrob left Riven on the battlefield instead of returning it to base, giving Arneej the opportunity to remove Riven on the following turn.

That play put Arneej in a favourable position, and he was able to close the game for a 2-1 victory.

### Round 7: Robot Aspirateur (Rengar), win 2-1

Round 7 brought Arneej up against Robot Aspirateur's Rengar deck.

This time, Arneej found many of the important cards he needed, including [Onslaught](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-081-onslaught/), [Alpha Strike](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-192-alpha-strike/), Rampage and Irelia.

The deciding **Game 3** included a particularly effective hidden Zhonya's Hourglass. His opponent had used a hand-disruption effect and seen Elnuk, but Arneej still managed to surprise him with Zhonya's.

Onslaught and Zhonya's were especially valuable throughout the series, allowing Arneej to trade efficiently into his opponent's units.

The 2-1 victory brought him to a 6-1 record with one round remaining.

### Round 8: Taal (Riven), win by concession

Arneej's final scheduled opponent was Taal, playing Riven.

Neither player could reach the top eight anymore, so they agreed to split their prize-wall tickets. Taal conceded the match, giving Arneej his seventh win of Nation Clash.

Although the final round was decided without playing the series, the result completed an excellent tournament run.

## A 7-1 finish and Belgium's top point earner

Arneej finished Nation Clash with a 7-1 record and 21 match points, making him Belgium's highest individual point earner in the event.

The result also earned him a gold medal, a reward he had not expected after the disappointment of missing Day 2 in the main event.

What started with a 3-3 finish on Friday ended with seven wins on Saturday, including several close series and important decisions.

Arneej also [shared his Nation Clash result on X](https://x.com/ArneejTCG), while Riftbound Organized Play posted [a Nation Clash standings update](https://x.com/riftboundop/status/2119353698501472486/photo/1) during the weekend.

For Arneej, the European Championship weekend ended on a much more positive note than it had started.
