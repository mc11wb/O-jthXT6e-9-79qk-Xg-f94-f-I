Guukboii's Riftbound European Championship in Stuttgart did not start as planned. After losing his first two matches, the Frites Esports Club player needed a strong recovery to keep his tournament alive.

Playing [Kennen, Heart of the Tempest](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-155-kennen-heart-of-the-tempest/), Guukboii faced twelve rounds of competitive Riftbound across November 6 and 7. You can find his [full Kennen decklist on Riftdecks](https://riftdecks.com/riftbound-metagame/deck-kennen-heart-of-the-tempest-345244).

Here is how he handled the difficult matchups, important decisions and unexpected moments throughout the weekend.

## Day 1: fighting back from a 0-2 start

### Round 1: DSG AwesumRosum (Kennen), loss 1-2

Guukboii opened against DSG AwesumRosum, whom he described as the world's fifth-ranked player. Both were running Kennen, making the first round a mirror match.

**Game 1** went Guukboii's way. AwesumRosum tried to establish a hold with [Tail-Cloaked Matriarch](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-104-tail-cloaked-matriarch/) and [Tornado Warrior](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-099-tornado-warrior/), but Guukboii had [Gust](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-169-gust/) ready. The response left his opponent struggling to recover.

In **Game 2**, AwesumRosum found an early Decree for Guukboii's Kennen and quickly filled his trash to enable [Rhasa, the Sunderer](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-195-rhasa-the-sunderer/). Game 3 was similarly difficult, with multiple Decrees disrupting Guukboii's plans during the midgame. Despite winning the opener, he lost the match 1-2.

### Round 2: Cramo (Jayce), loss 0-2

Guukboii considered Jayce a favourable matchup for Kennen, but admitted he lacked practice against the deck.

In **Game 1**, he opened aggressively with [Ride the Wind](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-173-ride-the-wind/) and [Nocturne, Horrifying](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-194-nocturne-horrifying/) from [Lightning Rush](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-156-lightning-rush/). He scored two points on turn one despite going second, but recycled back to zero runes. Cramo then played [Dazzling Aurora](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-160-dazzling-aurora/) on turn three, followed by multiple [Elder Dragons](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-118-elder-dragon/). Guukboii could not close the game before they took over.

**Game 2** followed a similar pattern. Even after playing all six Lightning Rushes, Guukboii could not find the gear removal he needed. Cramo once again found Aurora on turn three and eventually brought out all three Elder Dragons, securing a 2-0 victory.

### Round 3: DSG Prismaticism (Kennen), win 2-1

Another Kennen mirror awaited Guukboii, this time against DSG Prismaticism, described in his recap as the world's top-ranked player.

In **Game 1**, Prismaticism set up a hold with Matriarch and a hidden card. Guukboii had Gust ready and managed to make a favourable trade, maintaining his lead until the end.

**Game 2** went differently. Prismaticism found an early Nocturne through [Trifarian Captain](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-129-trifarian-captain/) and later used Decree to disrupt Guukboii's hold.

In **Game 3**, Guukboii found an early Nocturne and built a significant lead. Looking back, however, he felt he played too cautiously near the end. Instead of committing more resources, he repeatedly accepted equal trades, giving his opponent another chance to recover.

Fortunately, Prismaticism did not have the right hand to punish that decision. Guukboii secured his first match win.

### Round 4: Willy (Kai'Sa), win 2-1

Against Willy's Kai'Sa, Guukboii took a more patient approach. In **Game 1**, he kept [Last Rites](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-150-last-rites/) and Lightning Rush during his mulligan, developing gradually until he had enough mana to establish his value engine.

**Game 2** was more difficult. Willy found [Falling Star](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-029-falling-star/) into [Singularity](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-105-singularity/), slowing Guukboii down enough to win. Afterwards, Guukboii considered whether playing a two-cost unit instead of Vex might have been better, although another Falling Star could still have punished that decision.

In **Game 3**, Guukboii was ahead on points, but Willy had [Brynhir Thundersong](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-026-brynhir-thundersong/) with a hidden [Temporal Breach](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-066-temporal-breach/). When Temporal Breach was activated in response to Lightning Rush, Guukboii effectively lost a turn despite having ten runes available.

Willy did not find a second Temporal Breach, however, and Guukboii eventually overwhelmed him with multiple Rhasas.

### Round 5: MANA ominousmilk (Riven), win 2-0

Guukboii's fifth round was his quickest match of the day.

In **Game 1**, he won the dice roll and opened with Captain into Nocturne on turn one. His strong holds kept ominousmilk away from the battlefield, making it difficult to set up [Astral Heron](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ven-044-astral-heron/) plays.

**Game 2** followed a similar pattern, with Nocturne coming from Lightning Rush alongside two Seals. The entire match lasted approximately 20 to 25 minutes, bringing Guukboii to a 3-2 record.

### Round 6: MERC Koopastar (Riven), win 2-1

The final round of Day 1 brought Guukboii face-to-face with MERC Koopastar, the Orlando Showdown winner.

In **Game 1**, Koopastar scored on [Star Spring](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-215-star-spring/) with Scuttle Crab before attempting a greedy [Stellacorn Herder](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-048-stellacorn-herder/) play. Guukboii punished it with Gust during the Star Spring trigger, leaving Koopastar too far behind to recover.

**Game 2** was closer. Koopastar had [Defy](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-045-defy/) ready for a crucial [Star-Crossed](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-128-star-crossed/), and Guukboii could not find another opportunity to turn the game around.

Everything came down to **Game 3**, with only eight minutes remaining. Both players agreed to play quickly because a draw would not help either of them. On turn three, Guukboii used Star-Crossed on his Captain and Koopastar's Riven before replaying Captain. He then revealed three Nocturnes among the cards from the Captain play, an extremely unlikely moment that helped him secure the victory.

Guukboii finished Day 1 with four consecutive wins and a 4-2 record, qualifying for Day 2.

## Day 2: fighting for a top-58 finish

### Round 7: DiamondHat (Riven), loss 0-2

Guukboii opened Day 2 against DiamondHat, who would finish first in the Swiss standings.

It was a difficult match from the beginning. DiamondHat played extremely well, while Guukboii struggled to find the opportunities he needed. Whenever he tried to establish a hold using [Switcheroo](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-145-switcheroo/) or [Bedeviling Stranger](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-107-bedeviling-stranger/), DiamondHat had Gust ready.

Looking back, Guukboii felt the 0-2 loss came down to his opponent's strong interactive hand and excellent execution.

### Round 8: Kai'Sa, win 2-1

In **Game 1**, Guukboii's opponent found two Falling Stars, while he struggled to draw Lightning Rush or relevant units. Without enough cards in his trash, he was forced to play expensive Rhasas, which were immediately removed.

**Game 2** went much better. Guukboii found a strong opening, and his opponent did not draw any Falling Stars, allowing him to win quickly.

The deciding **Game 3** was a long battle. Guukboii was behind on points, but his opponent was running low on cards. He focused on generating value and forcing Kai'Sa to spend resources, eventually allowing his opponent to reach seven points.

By then, however, their hand was empty. Guukboii reached six points and established a strong hold. His opponent drew additional cards but failed to find the answer they needed, allowing Guukboii to close the game.

### Round 9: Riven, loss 0-2

In **Game 1**, Guukboii lost the dice roll and opened with [Reckless Innovator](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-105-reckless-innovator/). His opponent scored with a two-cost unit before developing their own Innovator on Star Spring, leaving one rune available. With [Traveling Merchant](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-185-traveling-merchant/) in hand, Guukboii decided to test whether his opponent had Gust. They did, forcing him to replay Innovator alongside Merchant.

Looking back, he felt developing Kennen and Merchant while staying in base might have been the better decision. Without finding an early Nocturne, he struggled to stop Riven's pressure.

**Game 2** followed a similar pattern. Guukboii missed his two-cost opening, and a risky hold was punished by a combination involving Blade and [Battle Preparations](https://riftdb.gg/cards/RAD-032/).

### Round 10: Kai'Sa, win 2-1

Guukboii faced Kai'Sa again in Round 10, this time in a series full of unusual plays.

In **Game 1**, he started strongly, leading on points and development with Rhasa equipped with Last Rites. However, holding a battlefield felt too risky because of Watcher, so he retreated to base while leading 4-2. His opponent then played two [Time Warps](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-122-time-warp/), jumping from two to eight points.

**Game 2** went much better. Guukboii used two Nocturnes from his opening hand as additional runes through [Ruined Monastery](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-166-ruined-monastery/). He later found Seals and Lightning Rushes, building an advantage through Innovator and Last Rites that his opponent could no longer match.

In **Game 3**, an early [Vex, Apathetic](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-150-vex-apathetic/) and Nocturne from Captain put Guukboii ahead. Later, he had a Rhasa on each battlefield, with a hidden [Edge of Night](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-139-edge-of-night/) on one of them.

His opponent played Falling Star on both Rhasas before moving in to eliminate one. Suspecting [Smoke Screen](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-093-smoke-screen/), Guukboii revealed Edge of Night on the other battlefield. His read was correct, and his opponent responded with Smoke Screen.

This opened a window for Guukboii to use Ride the Wind and move his remaining Rhasa to the empty battlefield, preventing Kai'Sa from conquering. He pushed to six points and threatened lethal on the following turn. His opponent attempted Singularity, but Guukboii had [Abandon](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-131-abandon/) ready to secure the victory.

### Round 11: Rek'Sai, win 2-1

**Game 1** started poorly. An early Falling Star cleared Guukboii's base, leaving him behind on points and unable to defend against the Might his opponent had built.

In **Game 2**, an early Nocturne from Captain gave Guukboii the extra Might he needed. He built a point lead, and once his Rhasas entered the board, the game ended quickly.

In **Game 3**, Guukboii went second and opened with a two-cost unit while keeping Gust available. When his opponent tried to score with [Carrion Dredger](https://riftbound.gg/cards/unl-153-carrion-dredger/), Guukboii responded with Gust, slowing the game enough to establish a stronger board.

His opponent still had a possible way back with exactly Decree and Tracker, but did not have the required cards. Guukboii eventually broke through a large hold across both battlefields and secured his seventh match win.

### Round 12: Riven, win 2-0

Guukboii's final round came with an important condition: a draw would not be enough for either player to secure a top-58 finish. Both needed a victory.

**Game 1** started slowly. His opponent scored several early points before Guukboii could establish a proper defence. He managed to slow Riven down with Star-Crossed, gradually building Might and taking favourable trades.

Eventually, Riven reached seven points, but Guukboii had complete control of both battlefields. With around 38 minutes remaining, his opponent spent almost seven minutes looking for a way through the defence before conceding.

That left roughly 30 minutes for Game 2, putting his opponent under pressure to win both remaining games within the time limit.

In **Game 2**, Guukboii took an early risk by playing Kennen onto [Hunters' Circle](https://riftbound.gg/cards/rad-163-hunters-circle/) on turn two and hiding a card. His opponent chose to develop on the other battlefield instead.

With Merchant already in play and Lightning Rush available, Guukboii quickly filled his trash and played an early Rhasa. Both players eventually held one battlefield each, slowing the game. Guukboii kept applying pressure with [Fizz, Trickster](https://riftbound.gg/cards/sfd-140-fizz-trickster/), repeating Star-Crossed and [Rebuke](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-172-rebuke/) against Astral Heron plays.

With fewer than ten minutes remaining, Guukboii was ahead but still far from winning. Rather than risk a draw, he pulled back slightly, allowing his opponent to take both battlefields and the point lead while still forcing them to spend resources.

The decision created an opportunity to strike back. Guukboii moved in with Rhasa, baiting his opponent's Stranger. When the Astral Heron trigger followed, he responded with Star-Crossed on Rhasa and Heron.

His opponent was forced to reveal [Zhonya's Hourglass](https://riftbound.gg/cards/ogn-077-zhonyas-hourglass/), but there were no useful units left to save. Guukboii reclaimed both battlefields, removed most of his opponent's board and reached seven points, setting up a double hold.

With only minutes remaining, his opponent could not break through the defence. Guukboii won 2-0, securing his place inside the top 58.

## From a 0-2 start to a top-25 finish

Guukboii finished the European Championship's Swiss rounds with an 8-4 record, placing 22nd in the published final standings. His final victory was particularly important, as poor tiebreakers meant he could not safely agree to an intentional draw.

The result earned him a top-58 prize selection, and Guukboii went home with a metal Vi, Piltover Enforcer card.

After a difficult start, he had fought his way back into Day 2 and finished among the top 25 players in Stuttgart. You can see the [complete European Regional Championship standings on BoundRift](https://boundrift.com/events/1227032).
