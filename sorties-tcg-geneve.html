How to read this calendar

We are supplied by the official distributors: a set is in the rails on release day, in all eight machines. It goes fast, the most anticipated sets empty within forty-eight hours. The only reliable way to know what is left is the Live Stock page, which reads inventory straight from connected machines.

Dates marked to be confirmed are windows announced by the publisher without a fixed day; we update them as soon as they are official.

Frequently asked questions

When is the next Pokémon set out?
Mega Evolution: Delta Reign releases on 6 November 2026, headlined by Mega Rayquaza ex. It is the last Mega Evolution set of the year. The 30th-anniversary set, 30th Celebration, came out on 16 September.
When does One Piece OP-18 release?
November 2026. Bandai has confirmed the month but not the day; retailers point to 20 November. The EB-05 extra booster lands first, at the end of October.
When does Riftbound Radiance release?
23 October 2026, after a Pre-Rift week from the 16th to the 22nd. 180 cards including 66 Showcase. The following set, Legacy, is dated 29 January 2027.
Is a set available in your machines on release day?
Yes. We are supplied by the official distributors and new releases go into the rails on release day. The Live Stock page shows in real time what is in each machine.
How can I be told when something arrives?
The request button on the licences page records your interest in a licence. Requests steer allocations and restocking.
