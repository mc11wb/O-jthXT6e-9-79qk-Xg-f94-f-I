      Frequently asked questions

      Is there a Pokémon card vending machine in Eaux-Vives?
      Not yet. The nearest one is inside La Poste de Rive, 1.2 km from Eaux-Vives, roughly 15 minutes on foot or 5 minutes by bike.
      How do I pay?
      Cash, with change returned in both coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless card and smartphone payment. The product drops instantly.
      What are the opening hours?
      Those of the Swiss Post branch hosting the machine. They are listed on the Rive page, with a live open or closed indicator.
      When will you have a machine in Eaux-Vives?
      The network grows with demand. Tell us where you would like one through the expansion page, every request is counted.
      Are the cards genuine?
      Yes. Every product is factory-sealed and comes from official distributors.
