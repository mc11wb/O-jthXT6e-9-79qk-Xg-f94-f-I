Frequently asked questions

Is there a Pokémon card vending machine in Champel?
Not yet. The nearest one is inside La Poste de Plainpalais, 1.2 km from Champel, roughly 15 minutes on foot or 5 minutes by bike.
How do I pay?
Cash, with change returned in both coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless card and smartphone payment. The product drops instantly.
What are the opening hours?
Those of the Swiss Post branch hosting the machine. They are listed on the Plainpalais page, with a live open or closed indicator.
When will you have a machine in Champel?
The network grows with demand. Tell us where you would like one through the expansion page, every request is counted.
Are the cards genuine?
Yes. Every product is factory-sealed and comes from official distributors.
