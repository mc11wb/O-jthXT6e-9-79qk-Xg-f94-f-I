Where to buy Union Arena cards in Geneva

One game, a dozen anime. Bleach, Hunter x Hunter, Jujutsu Kaisen, Attack on Titan or Code Geass face off in the same system, and Bandai ships a new booster almost every month.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed Union Arena boosters, one booster per anime licence. Attack on Titan Vol.2 and Re:ZERO released on 18 September, the NIKKE Precious Booster lands on 23 October, Bleach Vol.3 on 13 November and My Hero Academia on 11 December.

The schedule is the densest in all of TCG, which forces a choice: we prioritise the licences most requested in Geneva, Bleach, Jujutsu Kaisen and Hunter x Hunter, and rotate the others.

One game to learn

Union Arena’s appeal is economic as much as playful: a fan of several anime has one system to master and can play a Bleach deck against a Hunter x Hunter deck. It is also the simplest way into licences that will never get their own TCG.

Paying and collecting

Cash with change given in coins and notes, TWINT, or contactless by card and phone. The product drops immediately, with no account to create and no app to install.

Frequently asked questions
