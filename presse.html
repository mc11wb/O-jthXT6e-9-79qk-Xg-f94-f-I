Press room

Everything a journalist needs to write about BoosterStop. For interview, image or filming requests, email or call us directly.

Who we are

BoosterStop is a vending machine network dedicated to trading card games, operated by 247 Distribution SNC, a Geneva company. The machines sit inside Swiss Post branches across the canton of Geneva and work in self-service during the branch's opening hours.

Every product is factory-sealed and comes from official distributors. Nothing is weighed, searched, opened, sorted or resealed. Each machine's stock is read live and published on the site, so anyone can check what is inside before making the trip.
