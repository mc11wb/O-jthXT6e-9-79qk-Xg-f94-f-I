Where to buy Gundam cards in Geneva

Launched worldwide in July 2025, the game passed 500 million cards sold within months. In Geneva it is in all eight of our machines, and GD06 Stardust Trails will be there on 30 October.

What sits in the rails

Factory-sealed Gundam Card Game boosters, the current GD set first. Four new starter decks, ST11 to ST14, release on 25 September; we put them in the rails when the format allows.

Bandai keeps a brisk pace, a main set every two to three months, and European demand surprised everyone, the publisher included. The first waves were gone within hours.

A launch nobody saw coming

Gundam is a forty-five-year-old franchise with a loyal but quiet European following. The card game changed that: 700,000 participants at official events in year one, and a secondary market that structured itself within months. It is the licence that has grown most in our machines over the past year.
