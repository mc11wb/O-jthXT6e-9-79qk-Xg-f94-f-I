Where to buy NBA cards in Geneva

Prizm, Select and Donruss, the three collections that make the basketball card market, as sealed packs in our eight machines. The 2026-27 season tips off in October and the first releases follow.

What sits in the rails

Sealed packs from Panini’s NBA lines, Prizm first. The calendar follows the season: Donruss usually opens the year in December, Prizm and Select arrive over winter, and the June draft’s rookies are what everyone hunts.

Sports cards do not work like TCGs: no game, collecting only, and value concentrates on rookies and numbered parallels. A pack may hold nothing special or the card of the year.

Collections we carry

  • Panini PrizmThe reference, Silver parallels
  • Panini SelectThree tiers, Concourse, Premier, Courtside
  • Panini DonrussRated Rookies, first release of the season

Why Prizm rules

Since 2012 a rookie’s Silver Prizm has become the market’s yardstick: the card that gets compared, graded and tracked. Select and Donruss live around it. For a beginner, a Prizm pack is the best way to understand why NBA cards trade the way they do.
