Geneva is one of the most international cities in the world — and collectors don't all start in the same language. That's why our machines stock the same games across several languages, so you can find the version that actually matters to you.

🧩

Keep your collection consistent

Started your collection in Japanese? Kept every card in English since day one? You shouldn't have to switch language halfway through. We stock across languages so your binder stays coherent.

🌏

Read the cards you play

Whether you speak French, Mandarin, English, German, Spanish or Portuguese, playing is simply better when you can read your own cards — especially for younger players just getting into a game.

Some sets, arts and print runs only ever release in certain markets. Stocking Japanese and Chinese product means you can reach releases that never make it to Swiss shelves.

Are the products really authentic and untouched?
Yes. Everything is 100% sealed and comes directly from official distributors. We never weigh, search, tamper with or hand-pick packs. What goes in the machine is exactly as it left the manufacturer.
Why vending machines instead of a regular shop?
Convenience and 24/7 access matter, but our real goal is visibility: giving as many TCG licenses as possible a place in front of players — including the ones you rarely find on shelves — right where people already are.
Which games can I find in the machines?
Pokémon, One Piece, Dragon Ball Super, Riftbound: LoL, Magic: The Gathering, Disney Lorcana, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Naruto and more. The exact selection varies by machine — check the Live Stock page to see what's inside each one right now.
What languages are the cards in?
French, English, Japanese, Chinese, German, Spanish and Portuguese depending on the set and the machine. Collectors can keep their collection consistent in one language, or hunt market-exclusive sets from Japan and China.
What payment methods are accepted?
Cash (with change returned in coins and notes) and contactless payment by card or smartphone. Simple, fast, no app required.
A machine ate my money or a product is stuck — what do I do?
Contact us through the form below or by email with the machine location and approximate time — we monitor every machine remotely and will make it right quickly.
