Started your collection in Japanese? Kept every card in English since day one? You shouldn't have to switch language halfway through. We stock across languages so your binder stays coherent.

Trust & transparency

Sealed, official, no surprises.

Buying from a machine shouldn't mean buying blind. Here's exactly how we operate — and why we built BoosterStop the way we did.

🔒

100% sealed

Every booster, display and box is factory-sealed. Nothing is ever weighed, searched, opened or altered — products go straight from our distributors into the machine.

📦

Official sourcing

We source exclusively from the official distributors and authorized suppliers of each TCG. No grey market, no resealed product — ever.

🎥

Full transparency

We show as much as we can on our socials: restocking, sourcing, deliveries and openings. What you see online is what lands in the machine.

Are the products really authentic and untouched?
Yes — absolutely. Everything is 100% sealed and comes directly from official distributors. We never weigh, search, tamper with or hand-pick packs. What goes in the machine is exactly as it left the manufacturer.
Why vending machines instead of a regular shop?
Convenience and 24/7 access matter, but that's not the whole point. Our real goal is visibility: we want to give as many TCG licenses as possible a place in front of players — including the ones you rarely find on shelves — right where people already are.
Which games can I find in the machines?
Pokémon, One Piece, Dragon Ball Super, Riftbound: LoL, Magic: The Gathering, Disney Lorcana, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Naruto and more — with several universes available in each machine at the same time. The line-up evolves with new releases and demand.
In which languages are the cards available?
Several. We stock the same games across multiple languages — French, English, Japanese, Chinese, German, Spanish and Portuguese among others — for example Pokémon in Japanese, Chinese or French, and One Piece in English. This lets you keep a collection consistent in the language you started with, read the cards you actually play, and reach sets that only release in certain markets. Available languages vary by set and by machine, so check the Live Stock page to see what's in each one right now.
How do I pay?
We accept cash — with change returned in both coins and notes — as well as contactless card and smartphone payments. Choose your product, pay, and it drops instantly; the pack-opening experience starts right there at the machine.
Can I check stock before going?
Yes. Our Live Stock page shows real-time inventory for each machine. If something you want is missing, you can request a restock or a new product directly — we adjust our selection based on what you ask for.
